Data Type Mapping for Migration from Teradata to DWS
====================================================

.. table:: **Table 1** Data type mapping for migration from Teradata to DWS

   +-----------------------------------------------------+--------------------------------+--------------------------+
   | Teradata Data Type                                  | DWS Data Type                  | Mapping Supported or Not |
   +=====================================================+================================+==========================+
   | BIGINT                                              | BIGINT                         | Yes                      |
   +-----------------------------------------------------+--------------------------------+--------------------------+
   | BYTEINT                                             | SMALLINT                       | Yes                      |
   +-----------------------------------------------------+--------------------------------+--------------------------+
   | DECIMAL [(n[,m])]                                   | DECIMAL [(n[,m])]              | Yes                      |
   +-----------------------------------------------------+--------------------------------+--------------------------+
   | DOUBLE PRECISION                                    | DOUBLE PRECISION               | Yes                      |
   +-----------------------------------------------------+--------------------------------+--------------------------+
   | FLOAT                                               | DOUBLE PRECISION               | Yes                      |
   +-----------------------------------------------------+--------------------------------+--------------------------+
   | INT / INTEGER                                       | INTEGER                        | Yes                      |
   +-----------------------------------------------------+--------------------------------+--------------------------+
   | NUMBER / NUMERIC                                    | NUMERIC                        | Yes                      |
   +-----------------------------------------------------+--------------------------------+--------------------------+
   | NUMBER(n[,m])                                       | NUMERIC (n[,m])                | Yes                      |
   +-----------------------------------------------------+--------------------------------+--------------------------+
   | REAL                                                | REAL                           | Yes                      |
   +-----------------------------------------------------+--------------------------------+--------------------------+
   | SMALLINT                                            | SMALLINT                       | Yes                      |
   +-----------------------------------------------------+--------------------------------+--------------------------+
   | CHAR[(n)] / CHARACTER [(n)]                         | CHAR(n)                        | Yes                      |
   +-----------------------------------------------------+--------------------------------+--------------------------+
   | CLOB                                                | CLOB                           | Yes                      |
   +-----------------------------------------------------+--------------------------------+--------------------------+
   | LONG VARCHAR                                        | TEXT                           | Yes                      |
   +-----------------------------------------------------+--------------------------------+--------------------------+
   | VARCHAR(n) / CHAR VARYING(n) / CHARACTER VARYING(n) | VARCHAR(n)                     | Yes                      |
   +-----------------------------------------------------+--------------------------------+--------------------------+
   | DATE                                                | DATE                           | Yes                      |
   +-----------------------------------------------------+--------------------------------+--------------------------+
   | TIME [(n)]                                          | TIME [(n)]                     | Yes                      |
   +-----------------------------------------------------+--------------------------------+--------------------------+
   | TIME [(n)] WITH TIME ZONE                           | TIME [(n)] WITH TIME ZONE      | Yes                      |
   +-----------------------------------------------------+--------------------------------+--------------------------+
   | TIMESTAMP [(n)]                                     | TIMESTAMP [(n)]                | Yes                      |
   +-----------------------------------------------------+--------------------------------+--------------------------+
   | TIMESTAMP [(n)] WITH TIME ZONE                      | TIMESTAMP [(n)] WITH TIME ZONE | Yes                      |
   +-----------------------------------------------------+--------------------------------+--------------------------+
   | PERIOD(DATE)                                        | daterange                      | Yes                      |
   +-----------------------------------------------------+--------------------------------+--------------------------+
   | PERIOD(TIME [(n)])                                  | tsrange [(n)]                  | Yes                      |
   +-----------------------------------------------------+--------------------------------+--------------------------+
   | PERIOD(TIME WITH TIME ZONE)                         | tstzrange                      | Yes                      |
   +-----------------------------------------------------+--------------------------------+--------------------------+
   | PERIOD(TIMESTAMP [(n)])                             | tsrange [(n)]                  | Yes                      |
   +-----------------------------------------------------+--------------------------------+--------------------------+
   | PERIOD(TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE)                    | tstzrange                      | Yes                      |
   +-----------------------------------------------------+--------------------------------+--------------------------+
   | BLOB[(n)]                                           | blob                           | Yes                      |
   +-----------------------------------------------------+--------------------------------+--------------------------+
   | BYTE[(n)]                                           | bytea                          | Yes                      |
   +-----------------------------------------------------+--------------------------------+--------------------------+
   | VARBYTE[(n)]                                        | bytea                          | Yes                      |
   +-----------------------------------------------------+--------------------------------+--------------------------+
